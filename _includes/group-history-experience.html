Where our story begins

Three Groups grew side by side

Before today's Group shared one name, 1st, 2nd and 3rd Bundoora each had their own scarf, leaders and memories. Through the 1970s and 1980s their stories ran alongside one another, often crossing through the same families, district events and friendships.

Around 1990 those separate trails became the Bundoora Scout Group we know today. The pages and photographs left behind bring back only part of the journey, but the people in them carry the story across more than six decades.

1961 to about 1990

Three trails converge

Blue edged with orange, pale yellow and maroon, and a changing 3rd Bundoora scarf made each Group distinct. By about 1990, one maroon scarf marked the beginning of their shared chapter.
