Banyule District recognised three Scout leaders for a combined century of service at its annual Scout Hall event at Eaglemont Scout Hall.

District Commissioner Ken Youl had served since 1959. Trainer Rosemary Barnes had spent 25 years in Scouting. [Don Porteous](/about/history/people/donald-porteous) received a 50-year service award after roles with 1st Bundoora, Banyule District and service in New South Wales.

*First published in the Heidelberg Leader, 24 February 2004. Archive copy supplied by Scout Heritage Victoria.*
