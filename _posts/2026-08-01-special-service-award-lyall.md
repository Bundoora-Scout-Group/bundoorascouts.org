On World Scout Day, 1 August 2026, Scouts Australia announced that Lyall had received the [Special Service Award](https://scouts.com.au/about/what-is-scouting/awards/).

Lyall leads our Joey Scouts through their first years in Scouting. He gives our youngest members a welcoming place to try new activities, make friends, and grow in confidence. That work takes patience, preparation, and a steady commitment to each youth member.

The Special Service Award recognises a noteworthy contribution to a Scouting role. Lyall's service supports our Joey Scout section and helps families begin their Scouting journey at Bundoora.

Congratulations, Lyall, and thank you for the care and time you give to our Joey Scouts and the Group.
