Around 1,800 Scouts, Guides and leaders prepared for Jamborella Two at Gilwell Park from 28 December 1980 to 6 January 1981. The program included flying, canoeing, cycling, motor cycling, skating, arts, crafts and raft building.

A profile on the same page follows [Michael Downes](/about/history/people/michael-downes) from Assistant Scout Leader at 1st Bundoora in 1967 and Scout Leader in 1968 into later district, area, training and publication roles. His service brought him a Long Service Decoration and Medal of Merit.

*By Mike Downes. First published in The Victorian Scout, December 1980. Archive copy supplied by Scout Heritage Victoria.*
