Former Broadford resident Ash Marks received the Baden-Powell Scout Award in May 2016. Her Scouting journey began as a Joey Scout and continued through Cubs, Scouts, Venturers and Rovers.

The award recognised projects in rock climbing, community development and personal growth, including a Try Scouting Day for 1st Broadford.

*First published in the Macedon Free Press, 28 June 2016. Archive copy supplied by Scout Heritage Victoria.*
