On Monday 31 August, youth members, families, leaders, and supporters gathered for Bundoora Scout Group's 2026 Annual Report and Presentation (ARAP).

The evening gave each section a chance to look back on the year and share some of the activities, adventures, and achievements that made it memorable. Our Cub Scouts took to the front of the hall to present their report and show families photo displays from their program.

Kate, our fantastic Cub Scout Leader, also received recognition for 10 years of service with Scouts Victoria. Thank you, Kate, for the time, care, and enthusiasm you have given to young people and Scouting over the past decade.

Thank you to every youth member who took part, and to the leaders, adult helpers, families, and committee members who supported the Group throughout the year. Your time and effort keep Scouting active in Bundoora.
