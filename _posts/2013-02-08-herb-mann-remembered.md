> Herb. Many thanks for your years of service to the 1st Bundoora Scout Group. From Leaders, Parents and Youth Members.

A family notice alongside the Group's tribute remembered years of practical help, from plastering garages to installing antennas.

Herb served with Bundoora from 1983 to 1991, then carried that experience into a district leadership role until 1993.

*Death notices dated 8 February 2013 and Scout Heritage Victoria, leader warrant register.*
